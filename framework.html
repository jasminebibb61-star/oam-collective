The OAM Design Framework

You’ve built a full life. Now let’s make it yours.

A structured process for the woman who is ready to stop managing her life—and start designing it with intention.

Express your interestExplore the process

What this is

We’re building something together.

By the end, you will not only feel different—you will have a tangible blueprint for your life that you can return to, refine, and live by.

This may be for you if

  • You have done everything right—and you are still carrying more than you should have to.
  • You are not just overwhelmed in the moment; you are overloaded at the foundation.
  • You do not need to manage it better. You need to redesign it intentionally.

The five-step process

Five steps. One personal blueprint.

Each step builds on the last, moving you from understanding your overload to designing a life you can actually live by.

01

Name Your Overload

We begin by getting honest—naming what is creating overload in your life beneath the surface. This is where your Overload Map is born.

02

Understand Your Environment

We examine the systems, relationships, and conditions you are moving through so you can understand what you are actually working with.

03

Reimagine What You Want

We stop managing and start imagining. What do you actually want—not what you are supposed to want? This becomes the foundation of your Personal Design Blueprint.

04

Design Your Life Intentionally

We turn everything you have named and imagined into a real, workable design for how you want to live—with intention, not just reaction.

05

Step Away and Reconnect

You take intentional space to step back, reset, and reconnect with yourself, leaving with a Solo Reset Plan and a blueprint you are ready to live by.

Who this is for

For the Overachiever Mom—in every form she comes in.

If you are carrying more than you should have to and are ready to design something different, this work was created with you in mind.

The Climber

She followed the path.

School, career, marriage, kids, climb. She built the life she was supposed to want, yet something underneath it still feels incomplete.

The Builder

She created her own path.

She turned every detour into a door and built something real. She knows she is capable of more and needs space to design what that looks like.

The Juggler

She is in the middle of it.

Kids, career, and a dream she keeps deferring. She has not stopped moving long enough to ask what she actually wants—and she is ready to find out.

The experience

A guided process that leads to something you can use.

This is not inspiration without application. The work is designed to help you understand what is driving the overload and create a personal plan for what changes next.
