What is Human Design?

The owner’s manual you were never given.

Human Design uses your birth date, time, and location to create a map of how you are uniquely designed to move through the world. Jasmine’s work focuses on translating that map into grounded, useful reflection for real life.

  • It reveals how you are designed to make decisions that feel aligned.
  • It offers language for the environments and ways of working that restore or drain you.
  • It reframes qualities you may have been told were flaws.
  • It opens a conversation that begins with your design, not someone else’s expectations.

What it is not

Not another thing to optimize.

Within the OAM world, Human Design has one purpose: helping you understand yourself well enough to design a life that fits.

The five energy types

Your type is a starting point—not the whole story.

Each type describes a distinct relationship to energy and engagement. None is better than another; each operates differently.

Manifestor

Here to initiate, act independently, and create impact.

Generator

Here to respond and build with sustainable energy when the work feels alive.

Manifesting Generator

Here to respond, move quickly, and follow a multi-passionate path.

Projector

Here to guide, see systems clearly, and thrive through recognition rather than hustle.

Reflector

Here to reflect the health of an environment with rare sensitivity and wisdom.

Why Human Design lives here

Self-awareness becomes more useful when it changes the way you design your life.

After you name the overload and understand the environment around you, your design offers another mirror—one that can help you notice where you may be working against yourself.
